Biochemistry-I

Explore the chemical substances and biochemical processes that are essential for the structure and function of living organisms.

Course Code: Bioc2051 5 ECTS Year II · Semester I

📚 Course Overview

Biochemistry-I introduces the chemical basis of life and the molecular components that make up living organisms.

It helps students understand important biomolecules, their structures, properties, and roles in biological systems.

Important: The specific topics and learning outcomes depend on the official course syllabus. Confirm your course outline with your department before adding course-specific materials.
