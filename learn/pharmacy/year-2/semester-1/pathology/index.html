Pathology

Understand diseases, their causes, mechanisms, and effects on the structure and function of the human body.

Course Code: Path2054 5 ECTS Year II Semester I

📖 Course Overview

About This Course

Pathology is the study of diseases. It examines the causes of diseases, how they develop, the structural changes they produce in cells and tissues, and their effects on normal body functions.

This course provides an important foundation for understanding disease processes and their relevance to pharmacy practice.
