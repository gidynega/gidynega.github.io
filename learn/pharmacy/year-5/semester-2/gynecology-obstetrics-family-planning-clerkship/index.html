Gynecology, Obstetrics and Family Planning Clerkship

This learning page supports Pharmacy students studying medication use and pharmaceutical care in reproductive health, obstetrics, gynecology, and family planning.

Course Code: Phar5282 3 ECTS 2 Weeks Year V · Semester II

📘 Course Overview

About this course: This clerkship provides an opportunity to apply pharmacy knowledge to medication-related care in reproductive and maternal health settings under appropriate supervision.


Potential study areas include medication safety during pregnancy and breastfeeding, family-planning medicines, contraception, medication counselling, identification of drug-related problems, and communication with healthcare professionals.


Important: These are general study areas, not a verified list of the official course modules. Follow the official Jimma University syllabus and your clinical supervisor's instructions.
