Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Clerkship

Explore pharmaceutical manufacturing, production processes, quality systems, documentation, and the principles involved in producing safe and quality medicines.

Course Code: Phar5283 5 ECTS 3 Weeks Year V · Semester II

📘 Course Overview

Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Clerkship introduces students to practical aspects of pharmaceutical production and the systems used to maintain medicine quality.


General study areas may include manufacturing processes, Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP), production records, quality assurance, quality control, packaging, labeling, and appropriate storage of pharmaceutical products.


Important: These are general study areas, not a verified list of official course modules. Follow your instructor's syllabus and the materials provided by your university.
