Community Pharmacy Clerkship

Develop practical knowledge and skills in community pharmacy practice, including prescription processing, dispensing, patient counseling, medication safety, and pharmaceutical care.

Course Code: Phar5284 5 ECTS Duration: 3 Weeks Year V · Semester II

📚 Course Overview

Community pharmacy is an important part of the healthcare system that provides access to medicines and pharmaceutical services within the community.

During this clerkship, students connect classroom knowledge with supervised pharmacy practice. General learning areas may include prescription processing, dispensing, patient counseling, medication safety, communication with patients, health promotion, and appropriate referral.

Follow the official course outline and instructions from your clerkship supervisor for the required learning activities and competencies.
