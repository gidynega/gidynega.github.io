Pediatric Clerkship

Welcome to the Pediatric Clerkship learning page. Explore study resources related to pharmaceutical care for infants and children, safe medication use, patient assessment, and collaboration with healthcare professionals.

Course Code: Phar5281 7 ECTS 4 Weeks Year V · Semester II

📘 Course Overview

About this course: Pediatric Clerkship is a supervised pharmacy practice rotation focused on applying pharmaceutical knowledge to medication use in infants and children.


Learning areas may include age-appropriate medication use, weight-based dose calculations, medication safety, identifying drug-related problems, monitoring treatment, and communicating with caregivers and healthcare professionals.


Important: Use your official Jimma University course materials and follow your clinical supervisor's instructions. Medication decisions for children require careful verification and professional supervision.
