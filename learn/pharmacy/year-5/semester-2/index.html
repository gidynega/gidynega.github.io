🏥 Year V — Semester II: Pharmacy Clerkship II

This section covers the second group of professional clerkships in the national Bachelor of Pharmacy curriculum.

Curriculum source: Nationally Harmonized Bachelor of Pharmacy Curriculum for Ethiopian Higher Education Institutions, May 2021.

1. Pediatric Clerkship

Course Code: Phar5281

Credit: 7 ECTS

Duration: 4 weeks

Develop practical skills in pharmaceutical care for infants, children, and adolescents, including medication safety and age-appropriate medication use.

2. Gynecology, Obstetrics and Family Planning Clerkship

Course Code: Phar5282

Credit: 3 ECTS

Duration: 2 weeks

Learn about medication-related care in women's health, pregnancy, obstetrics, gynecology, and family planning.

3. Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Clerkship

Course Code: Phar5283

Credit: 5 ECTS

Duration: 3 weeks

Gain practical exposure to pharmaceutical manufacturing, production processes, quality practices, and safe handling of pharmaceutical products.

4. Community Pharmacy Clerkship

Course Code: Phar5284

Credit: 5 ECTS

Duration: 3 weeks

Learn about community pharmacy services, prescription processing, patient counseling, responsible medicine use, and supporting public health.

📊 Semester II Summary

Total Courses Listed: 4

Total Credits: 20 ECTS

Total Clerkship Duration: 12 weeks

📌 Important Curriculum Note

The May 2021 national curriculum states that Year V Semester I and Semester II clerkships are organized as a year-based final-year attachment, with student assessment at the end of the year. The semester pages on this website are for organizing learning materials; they do not imply separate official semester assessments.
