🏥 Year V — Semester I: Pharmacy Clerkship I

Welcome to Year V of the Bachelor of Pharmacy (BPharm) program. This year focuses on practical pharmacy training through clinical and professional clerkships.

Curriculum source: Nationally Harmonized Bachelor of Pharmacy Curriculum for Ethiopian Higher Education Institutions, May 2021.

1. Ambulatory Care Clerkship

Course Code: Phar5271

Credit: 5 ECTS

Duration: 3 weeks

Learn how pharmacists provide care to patients who receive healthcare without being admitted to a hospital. Training may involve medication counseling, reviewing prescriptions, and supporting the safe and effective use of medicines.

2. Drug Information Service Clerkship

Course Code: Phar5272

3. Internal Medicine Clerkship

Course Code: Phar5273

Credit: 7 ECTS

Duration: 4 weeks

Gain practical experience in pharmaceutical care for patients with medical conditions. Focus on understanding medication therapy, identifying drug-related problems, and supporting appropriate treatment under clinical supervision.

📌 Important Curriculum Note

According to the May 2021 national curriculum, Year V Semester I and Semester II clerkships are organized as a year-based final-year attachment. Student assessment takes place at the end of the year. The semester headings on this website are for organizing the learning materials.
