Hospital Pharmacy Clerkship

Develop practical knowledge of hospital pharmacy services, medication management, medicine distribution, medication safety, and the pharmacist's contribution to patient care. Explore study resources, revision notes, and practice questions.

Course Code: Phar5274 7 ECTS Duration: 4 Weeks Year V · Semester I

📚 Course Overview

Hospital pharmacy focuses on the management and appropriate use of medicines within hospital settings.

This clerkship provides an opportunity to explore medicine procurement, inventory management, storage, distribution, medication-order review, dispensing systems, and medication safety.

Clinical reminder: Follow your official clerkship objectives, supervisor's instructions, institutional procedures, and applicable clinical guidelines. These topics are general learning areas, not a verified official course syllabus.
