🎯 Learning Objectives

During your learning and supervised clerkship, aim to understand how to:

  • Describe the organization and responsibilities of hospital pharmacy services.
  • Explain medication procurement, storage, distribution, and inventory management.
  • Understand medication-order review and dispensing procedures.
  • Identify common medication errors and strategies for preventing them.
  • Explain the principles of medication safety and reporting adverse drug reactions.
  • Understand the role of pharmacists in antimicrobial stewardship.
  • Communicate effectively with patients and healthcare professionals.
  • Apply professional ethics, confidentiality, and appropriate documentation.

📚 Learning Modules

🏥

Module 1: Hospital Pharmacy Services

Organization, responsibilities, workflow, and the pharmacist's role in hospital care.

📦

Module 2: Procurement and Inventory

Medicine selection, purchasing processes, stock control, and inventory records.

🌡️

Module 3: Storage and Distribution

Storage conditions, temperature monitoring, stock rotation, and medicine distribution.

📝

Module 4: Medication Orders

Prescription review, identifying potential problems, dispensing, and documentation.

🛡️

Module 5: Medication Safety

Medication errors, risk reduction, adverse drug reactions, and reporting systems.

🧪

Module 6: Special Medication Handling

Principles for handling high-alert medicines, hazardous medicines, and sterile products where applicable.

🤝

Module 7: Clinical Collaboration

Communication, medication information, antimicrobial stewardship, and teamwork.

📋

Module 8: Quality and Professional Practice

Standard operating procedures, quality improvement, confidentiality, and professional ethics.

Important: These are general study areas, not a verified official module list. Follow your lecturer's syllabus and hospital clerkship handbook for the required curriculum.

🩺 Clerkship Preparation

Before your hospital placement

  • Review the hospital's rules and the clerkship objectives.
  • Revise medication safety, prescription review, and storage principles.
  • Prepare a notebook for supervised learning activities and reflections.
  • Understand confidentiality and the limits of your student responsibilities.

During your placement

  • Observe hospital pharmacy procedures under appropriate supervision.
  • Ask questions when a procedure or medication-related decision is unclear.
  • Follow infection prevention, safety, and documentation requirements.
  • Never independently change or dispense a patient's medication outside your authorized role.

Patient safety first: This page is an educational aid. Follow your supervising pharmacist, hospital policies, and applicable clinical guidelines during real patient care.

✅ Self-Study Checklist

Use this checklist to track your preparation:

  • ☐ Explain the major functions of a hospital pharmacy.
  • ☐ Describe basic procurement and inventory processes.
  • ☐ Explain appropriate medicine storage and distribution.
  • ☐ Identify potential medication-order problems.
  • ☐ Discuss medication errors and patient-safety measures.
  • ☐ Explain the importance of documentation and confidentiality.
  • ☐ Review the official clerkship assessment requirements.
