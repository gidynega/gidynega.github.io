🎯 Learning Objectives

Use this list to guide your preparation:

  • Describe the organization and functions of hospital pharmacy services.
  • Explain medicine procurement, storage, inventory, and distribution.
  • Understand medication-order screening and dispensing procedures.
  • Identify medication errors and methods of preventing them.
  • Explain adverse drug reaction reporting and medication safety.
  • Understand the importance of antimicrobial stewardship.
  • Practice professional communication and accurate documentation.
  • Respect patient confidentiality, professional ethics, and supervision requirements.

Module 3: Storage and Distribution

Module 4: Medication Orders

Module 5: Medication Safety

Medication errors, adverse drug reactions, and risk-reduction strategies.

Drug information, communication, teamwork, and antimicrobial stewardship.

Documentation, standard procedures, confidentiality, and quality improvement.
