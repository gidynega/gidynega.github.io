Internal Medicine Clerkship

Explore clinical learning resources related to patient assessment, medical conditions, pharmacotherapy, medication safety, and pharmaceutical care during internal medicine rotations.

Course Code: Phar5273 7 ECTS Duration: 4 Weeks Year V · Semester I

📚 Course Overview

Internal medicine focuses on the assessment and management of adult patients with medical conditions.

General learning areas may include reviewing patient histories, understanding laboratory findings, evaluating medication regimens, identifying drug-related problems, monitoring treatment, and communicating with healthcare teams.

Clinical reminder: Follow the official clerkship objectives, current clinical guidelines, and your supervisor's instructions. These examples are not a verified official course syllabus. Patient-specific decisions must be made under appropriate clinical supervision.
