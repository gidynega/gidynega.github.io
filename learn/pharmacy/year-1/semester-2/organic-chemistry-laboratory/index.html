Organic Chemistry Laboratory

Develop practical laboratory skills through experiments involving organic compounds, their physical and chemical properties, identification, and reactions. Connect theoretical organic chemistry with practical pharmaceutical applications.

Year I Semester II 2 ECTS

Course Overview

This laboratory course introduces students to essential practical techniques used in organic chemistry. Students develop skills in laboratory safety, handling chemicals and equipment, carrying out experiments, observing reactions, recording results, and interpreting experimental findings.

Practical activities should always be performed under appropriate laboratory supervision and according to institutional safety procedures.
