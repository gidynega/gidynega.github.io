Communicative English Skill II

Develop effective English communication skills for academic, professional, and everyday situations. Strengthen your reading, writing, listening, speaking, and critical communication abilities.

Course Code: FLEn1012 Credit: 5 ECTS Year I • Semester II

📘 Course Overview

Communicative English Skill II focuses on improving students' ability to communicate clearly and confidently in English. It supports academic learning, effective interaction, professional communication, and the development of language skills required in higher education.

Course Code: FLEn1012
Credit Hours: 5 ECTS
Duration: 135 hours
Semester: Year I, Semester II
