Social Anthropology

Explore human societies, cultures, social relationships, beliefs, traditions, and institutions, and understand how social and cultural factors influence people's health and healthcare practices.

Year I Semester II 5 ECTS

📘 Course Overview

Welcome to Social Anthropology! This course introduces students to the study of human societies and cultures. It explores how people live, interact, organize communities, develop beliefs, and maintain social relationships. For pharmacy and healthcare students, understanding social anthropology is important because culture, religion, family, traditions, economic conditions, and community beliefs can influence health, illness, medicine use, and healthcare-seeking behavior.
