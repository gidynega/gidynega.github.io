Course Code: Phyc-1011

Course Code: SpSc 1011

Credit: Non-credited

Promote physical activity and fitness.

Total: 30 ECTS for the six credited courses. Physical Fitness is listed separately as non-credited.

Note: This is based on the national May 2021 curriculum. Course offerings may vary by university.
