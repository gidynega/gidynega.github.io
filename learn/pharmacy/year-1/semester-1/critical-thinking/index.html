Critical Thinking

Develop the ability to analyze information, evaluate evidence, identify assumptions, solve problems, and make logical, well-informed decisions in academic and everyday situations.

Course Code: LoCT 1011 Credit: 5 ECTS Year I • Semester I

📘 Course Overview

Critical Thinking focuses on developing the ability to think clearly, logically, independently, and objectively.

Students learn to examine arguments, distinguish facts from opinions, recognize errors in reasoning, evaluate evidence, and make appropriate decisions.

These skills are important in pharmacy because healthcare professionals must evaluate information, interpret evidence, solve problems, and make responsible decisions.
