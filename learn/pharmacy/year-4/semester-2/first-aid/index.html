First Aid

Welcome to your First Aid learning center. Explore learning materials about recognizing emergencies, providing immediate assistance, protecting patients from further harm, and understanding when professional medical help is needed.

Course Code: Nurs4245
Credits: 3 ECTS
Level: Year IV

Welcome, Future Healthcare Professional! 🎓

First aid is the immediate assistance given to someone who is injured or suddenly becomes ill before definitive medical care is available. Learning first aid helps healthcare students recognize emergencies, respond appropriately, and understand their responsibilities in protecting life and preventing further harm.
