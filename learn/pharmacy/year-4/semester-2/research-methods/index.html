Course Code: Phar4261 3 ECTS Year IV · Semester II

📚 Course Overview

Research Methods introduces the principles and processes used to plan, conduct, analyze, and communicate research.

Potential study areas include research questions, literature reviews, research designs, sampling, data collection, data analysis, research ethics, and scientific reporting.
