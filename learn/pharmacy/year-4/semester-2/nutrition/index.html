Welcome, Future Healthcare Professional! 🎓

Nutrition is the study of how the body obtains and uses nutrients for energy, growth, tissue repair, and maintaining health. Understanding nutrition helps healthcare professionals recognize nutritional problems, provide appropriate education, and understand how food, disease, and medicines may interact.
