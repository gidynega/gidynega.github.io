📖 Year III — Semester I Courses

Explore the courses listed in the May 2021 Ethiopian Bachelor of Pharmacy curriculum.

1. Integrated Physical Pharmacy and Pharmaceutics-II

Course Code: Phar3121

Credit: 7 ECTS

Study advanced principles of physical pharmacy and pharmaceutical formulation.

2. Practical Integrated Physical Pharmacy and Pharmaceutics-II

Course Code: Phar3122

Credit: 2 ECTS

Apply pharmaceutics principles through practical laboratory activities.

3. Pharmacology II

Course Code: Phar3131

Credit: 7 ECTS

Continue studying drug actions, therapeutic effects, and adverse effects.

4. Medicinal Chemistry-II

Course Code: Phar3141

Credit: 5 ECTS

Explore the chemical properties and structure–activity relationships of medicines.

5. Pharmaceutical Analysis-I

Course Code: Phar3151

Credit: 7 ECTS

Study analytical methods used to identify and measure pharmaceutical substances.

6. Integrated Therapeutics-I

Course Code: Phar3162

Credit: 7 ECTS

Develop skills in evaluating diseases and selecting appropriate drug therapy.

7. Physical Assessment

Course Code: Intm 3161

Credit: 2 ECTS

Learn fundamental patient assessment and clinical examination skills.

Note: The curriculum lists this as a co-requisite for Integrated Therapeutics-I.

Total: 37 ECTS

Based on the national May 2021 curriculum. Actual course offerings may vary by university.
