About Me 👋

🚀

Hello, I'm Gidy Nega!

My name is Gidy Nega and I was born 01/01/1996E.C at Ambuye town, Limmu Kossa Woreda,in Jimma zone that found in south western Ethiopia. I was from from my father Nega Kemal and my mom Zenati Jemal. I have totally 4 brothers and 2 sisters. My family is working on different source of income. So I was living with them and When I reached the appropriate age to start school, I enrolled in Ambuye Primary SchoolI.I completed Grades 1–4 at Ambuye Primary School. After that, my parents moved to the countryside for work, so I interrupted my education for one year and went with them. Later, I enrolled at Geenaa Qilee School, located in the rural area, where I completed my primary education from Grades 5–8. Since there was no secondary school (Grades 9–12) in that rural area, I returned to Ambuye town and completed my secondary education there. After completing Grade 12, I joined Jimma University. After joining the university, I took the freshman courses and then entered the health sciences field, specifically the Pharmacy program. Currently, in addition to studying Pharmacy, I am also pursuing knowledge in various other fields, including technology, trading, a Bachelor of Arts in Management, and creative skills. Now I am student with a growing with learning new things, solving problems, and turning ideas into practical projects.

My journey combines pharmaceutical knowledge with technology. I want to use what I learn to create useful digital solutions, share knowledge, and help people.

My vision is to become a skilled developer and future tech entrepreneur who builds innovative projects and makes a positive impact on the world.
